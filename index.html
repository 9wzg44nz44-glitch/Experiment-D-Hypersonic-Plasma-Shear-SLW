Hypersonic plasma-sheath cavitation, high shear, and MHD sheets as SLW / SW sources

Hypothesis

At Mach 5–20 in the upper atmosphere / lower ionosphere, shock heating ionizes air. The vehicle is wrapped in a sheath of free electrons (often ne ~ 1016–1020 m⁻³) that already blackouts ordinary radio. The sheath fluctuates with attitude, altitude, ablation, and the geomagnetic field.

First-class Hypersonic flow is high-shear flow: extreme velocity gradients in the boundary layer, shock-slip surfaces, wake shear layers, and MHD-forced sheets. Shear steepens ∇ne and ∇φ, drives thin v×B electric sheets and Hall charge separation, and imprints bursty, attitude-linked modulation on any longitudinal / scalar channel. Gradient strength scales with shear rate — higher shear → stronger predicted SLW / SW drive than a uniform sheath of the same average ne.

Hively EED sketch (educational · HYP; SI ledger form): gradient-driven current J ∝ ∇ρ (or ∇φ) → dynamical scalar C = ∇·A + c⁻² ∂tΦ , □C = −μ₀ (∂tρ + ∇·J) → longitudinal E → SLW channel (SLW flux falls as 1/r², Hively & Loebl 2019) SW limit: E = B = 0, C ≠ 0 (Ohmic σE² term off) Predicted: SLW ignores classical skin-effect cutoff that kills TEM through the sheath.

Discriminants

Pass / interesting

  • TEM-null path still shows tone / bursts.
  • Survives conductive barrier that kills TEM (skin-effect test).
  • Longitudinal / scalar polarization preference vs transverse.
  • Burst spectrum tracks Mach, AoA, control-surface events — not only altitude or mean ne.
  • Spatial peak along shear sheet / wake, not only stagnation cap.

Fail / boring

  • Signal dies with TEM loop / single-filar floor.
  • No correlation with shear kinematics.
  • Indistinguishable from sferics / ionospheric clutter after TEM rejection.
  • Only mean-ne scaling, no shear boost.

Risks / caveats

  • Ordinary plasma noise, sferics, and ionospheric clutter can fake “bursts.”
  • TEM rejection must be ruthless; leakage looks like a discovery.
  • This is a detection / exploitation hypothesis, not a demonstrated weapon or flight signature.
  • Lab analogs (pulsed plasma / spark + MHD coil + sheared flow) before any field geometry.
